## Section 2: 3-Point Fit Checklist (interactive pre-qualifier)

- `[Widget: Heading - H2]` The 3-Point Fit Checklist: Will a Straight Stair Lift Work for You?
- `[Widget: Text Editor]` Stand at the bottom of your stairs and answer three questions. If you can say yes to all three, a straight stair lift is very likely the right fit.

- `[Widget: Toggle]` (first item open by default)
  - **1. Do your stairs run in one straight line from bottom to top?**
    No turns, no curve, and no landing where the stairs change direction. A straight run is what lets us use a standard rail that's cut to length, which is why the price is lower and the install is quick.
    *Have a flat landing in the middle? You may still qualify with [two straight lifts](/stair-lifts/stairs-with-landings/).*
  - **2. Are all the steps the same rectangular shape?**
    Pie-shaped or triangular steps (called winders) mean the staircase turns, even if it looks nearly straight. Those need a custom curved rail.
  - **3. Can the rider sit down and stand up safely at the top and bottom?**
    The rider needs to be able to sit upright, and to get on and off the seat with or without a helper. The seat swivels at the top so you step off onto the floor, not onto the stairs. Most stair lifts need about 28 to 30 inches of stair width, and slim-rail models can sometimes fit narrower stairs.

## Section 8: FAQ

- `[Widget: Heading - H2]` Straight Stair Lift FAQ
- `[Widget: Toggle]`
  - **How long does straight stair lift installation take?** Typically two to four hours once the lift is on site. From first call to a working lift is usually a matter of days.
  - **Does the rail attach to the wall?** No. It attaches to the stair treads, so walls and railings are left alone.
  - **Can other people still use the stairs?** Yes. The seat, arms and footrest fold up when the lift isn't in use.
  - **What if the power goes out?** `[CONFIRM]` The lifts we supply run on rechargeable batteries, so they keep working during an outage.
  - **How much weight can a stair lift carry?** It depends on the model. Standard straight lifts typically carry around `[CONFIRM: 300 lbs]`, and heavier-duty models are available.
  - **My stairs have a landing. Am I out of luck?** Not at all. See how [two straight lifts](/stair-lifts/stairs-with-landings/) can replace one custom curved lift.
