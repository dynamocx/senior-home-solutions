## Section 2: How it works (3 columns)

- `[Widget: Heading - H2]` How Two Straight Stair Lifts Work Together

1. `[Widget: Icon Box]` **Ride the first lift** from the bottom floor up to the landing.
2. `[Widget: Icon Box]` **Cross the landing.** The seat swivels so you step off onto flat floor. Take a few steps, or transfer with a helper, to the second lift.
3. `[Widget: Icon Box]` **Ride the second lift** the rest of the way to the top.

- `[Widget: Text Editor]` Each lift is a standard straight stair lift with its own rail, seat and controls. Nothing is custom-built, which is where the savings come from.

## Section 4: Honest fit check (interactive pre-qualifier)

- `[Widget: Heading - H2]` Is the Two-Lift Solution Right for You?
- `[Widget: Text Editor]` Two lifts aren't right for everyone, and we'd rather tell you now. Check these three points.

- `[Widget: Toggle]`
  - **1. Is your landing flat and roomy?**
    The landing needs to be a level floor with space to step off one seat and reach the next. A landing made of pie-shaped steps won't work.
  - **2. Are both flights straight?**
    Each flight needs to run in a straight line. Flights can be different lengths.
  - **3. Can the rider take a few steps or transfer safely?**
    This is the important one. The rider has to get from one seat to the other, alone or with a caregiver. If standing and stepping isn't safe, even with help, a curved lift that keeps them seated may be the better choice, and we'll say so.

- 2 columns:
  - `[Widget: Icon Box]` **A good fit when:** the rider walks short distances, the landing is flat, and budget or timing matters.
  - `[Widget: Icon Box]` **Consider a curved lift when:** the rider can't transfer safely, or the stairs curve with no flat landing.

## Section 7: FAQ

- `[Widget: Heading - H2]` Stairs With Landings FAQ
- `[Widget: Toggle]`
  - **Can you put a stair lift on stairs with a landing?** Yes. You can use one custom curved lift, or two straight lifts with a transfer at the landing.
  - **Are two straight stair lifts really cheaper than one curved lift?** In most cases, yes, because nothing is custom-made. `[CONFIRM: savings claim]`
  - **How far is the walk between the two lifts?** Usually just a few steps across the landing. We plan where each lift parks to keep it as short as possible.
  - **Will two rails get in the way?** Each rail sits on its own flight, close to the wall side of the treads, and the seats fold up. The landing itself stays clear.
  - **What if the landing has a 90-degree turn?** That's the most common layout for this solution, as long as the landing is a flat floor.
  - **How fast can both lifts be installed?** Straight lifts are typically installed in two to four hours each. `[CONFIRM: both in one visit?]`
